FamilyTrack

A little help with everyday care

Keep everyday care information together, so you and the people you care with can follow what has happened and what is coming up.

What can I do in the app?

  • Keep care profiles for children or adults.
  • Record daily care, including food, fluids, sleep and medication.
  • Look back through the timeline and create care reports.
  • Set up reminders and share a workspace with authorised carers.

Already have an account?

Return to the FamilyTrack app and log in with your existing email address and password. If you have been invited to a family workspace, follow the instructions in your invitation.

Need a hand?

For help using FamilyTrack or trouble logging in, email hello@familytrack.care. Please avoid including sensitive care records in your first message.
